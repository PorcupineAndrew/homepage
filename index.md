## About Me

Hi! I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University, working with Prof. [Vyas Sekar](https://users.ece.cmu.edu/~vsekar/). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Tsinghua University, where I was co-advised by Prof. [Jianping Wu](https://www.internethalloffame.org/inductee/jianping-wu/) and Prof. [Qi Li](https://sites.google.com/site/qili2012/). My research focuses on <b>network security</b> and <b>AI for security</b>. I am particularly interested in developing practical, AI-driven approaches to address both enduring and emerging threats in Internet infrastructure and networked systems.
